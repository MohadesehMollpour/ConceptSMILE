# Responsible AI scope

ConceptSMILE is intended for research into concept-response reliability under controlled perturbation. Understandable concepts and high surrogate fidelity do not establish clinical safety, causal validity, useful clinical explanations or generalisation. It is not a diagnostic model, certification procedure or universal trust score.

The manuscript reports a small 40-image proof of concept and three broad concepts. Exact subset membership and several experiments cannot be recovered. Historical reference metrics have the limitations stated in scientific_provenance.md; none is presented here as independent clinical attribution validation. Model-derived masks are not expert truth, and generic removed-superpixel fraction is not measured concept-specific evidence.

Black masking can generate unrealistic inputs. Extractor quality, heuristic prompts, concept vocabulary and VLM tokenisation/inference choices constrain interpretation. No clinician-in-the-loop, prospective clinical or causal validation is established. Sanitised historical outputs are research records, not patient-specific clinical advice.

No medical images are distributed in this ZIP. Future users must obtain datasets independently, review provider terms, minimise personal data and record provenance. The software retains an all-rights-reserved notice; no new licence or clinical-use permission is granted.
