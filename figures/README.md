# Figure provenance and redistribution status

This directory contains figures presented in the ConceptSMILE manuscript. Their inclusion is intended to support documentation, traceability, and review of the reported study.

## Figure categories

- `framework/` contains manuscript Figures 1–7, which describe the ConceptSMILE pipeline, concept-based XAI background, reliability challenges, perturbation process, and evaluation framework.
- `results/` contains manuscript Figures 8–11, which present qualitative and quantitative outputs reported in the manuscript.

## Provenance status

These figures should not be interpreted as independently regenerated or reproduced experimental outputs unless the corresponding source data, scripts, and execution provenance are available and verified.

In particular:

- Figure 8 is a manuscript-presented qualitative output.
- Figure 9 is a manuscript-presented surrogate-fidelity comparison; the complete historical source data and exact target construction have not been fully recovered.
- Figures 10 and 11 are manuscript-presented robustness figures; the complete historical source data and experiment provenance have not been fully recovered.

## Image and third-party rights

Some figures contain retinal-image content or other graphical elements derived from the manuscript. The repository does not establish independent ownership or redistribution rights for every underlying image or third-party element.

The exact provenance, original image identifiers, and redistribution permissions for all embedded retinal-image content have not been fully verified in the current research record. Inclusion of these manuscript figures therefore does not imply that the underlying image assets are released under the repository's software rights statement.

No dataset is redistributed through this repository.

## Interpretation

The figures are retained as manuscript-presented research artefacts for transparency and traceability. They must not be described as independently reproduced results where the original source data or execution evidence is unavailable.

See `docs/audits/final-scientific-audit.md` and `docs/scientific_provenance.md` for the current evidence and provenance assessment.
