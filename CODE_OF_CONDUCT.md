# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-oriented research environment. Contributors must communicate professionally, welcome constructive review, respect privacy and dataset restrictions, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or deliberate scientific misrepresentation.

Report conduct concerns privately to the corresponding author at `m.mollapour-papkyadeh-2023@hull.ac.uk`. The maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the integrity of the project.
